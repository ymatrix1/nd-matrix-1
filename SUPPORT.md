# Getting help

- **Using the app:** see the [README](README.md), [Commands and zoom](docs/guides/commands.md) and [host compatibility](docs/HOST_COMPATIBILITY.md).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/ymatrix1/nd-matrix-1/issues/new?template=bug.yml) with your version, OS, host and steps.
- **An idea:** open a [feature request](https://github.com/ymatrix1/nd-matrix-1/issues/new?template=feature.yml).
- **Security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Rights-holder or legal concern:** see [docs/LEGAL.md](docs/LEGAL.md).

Never attach firmware, `.ns4b` banks, samples or vendor material.
